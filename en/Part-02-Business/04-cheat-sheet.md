# 04: Cheat Sheet

Reading time: 2 minutes. Review it before starting the exercise.

## The Six Questions

1. Who is the customer and who is the user?
2. What problem do we solve?
3. How does money move?
4. What are the core operations?
5. What rules govern the work?
6. Who are the other parties?

## Terms

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Business Domain | The field the project works in | E-commerce, clinics, delivery |
| Customer | Who pays | The company that bought the system |
| User | Who uses the system | An employee of that company |
| Value | The problem we solve | Shopping from home |
| Money Flow | Who pays whom, when, and how much | Customer, Gateway, Store |
| Core Process | The main journey from start to finish | Browse, Cart, Order, Pay, Ship |
| Business Rule | A policy governing an operation | Cancel only before shipping |
| External Party | An outside party the system depends on | Payment Gateway, shipping company |
| Order Status | A stage in an order's life | Pending, Paid, Shipped |
| COD | Cash on Delivery | Money collected by the courier |
| Question Log | Your questions for the Business | Up to which status can we cancel? |
