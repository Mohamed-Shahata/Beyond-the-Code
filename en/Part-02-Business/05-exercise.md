# 05: Exercise: Understand a New Business

Estimated time: 15 minutes

This time the domain is brand new, so you can try the six questions on a Business that is not E-commerce. Read the description and answer. Try not to open the solution until you finish.

---

## LaundryGo (a made-up name)

A laundry service that collects clothes from the customer's door and returns them clean. The customer requests a pickup time in the app. The courier collects the clothes and weighs them in front of the customer, and the price is calculated by weight and wash type. The clothes then go to the laundry, get washed, and come back to the customer.

Payment is cash to the courier or online. The laundry offers monthly subscriptions at a discount. If clothes get damaged, the customer is compensated. Notifications are sent to the customer's phone.

---

## Questions

1. Answer the six Business questions for this Business. If a question does not apply, write "not applicable" and why.
2. Draw the Order Statuses from pickup to delivery.
3. Write 5 questions for the Business that the description leaves unclear, and explain why each matters.
4. Extra thinking: if this were an internal company system with no money at all (a leave system, say), what would you replace the money question with?
